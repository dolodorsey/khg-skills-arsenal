# KHG Ambassador & Influencer Command

## Mission
Build a measurable creator/ambassador distribution network for one entity, with brand fit and conversion quality prioritized over follower count.

## Candidate score
Score audience quality, geographic fit, content quality, category relevance, engagement authenticity, brand safety, conversion potential, consistency, relationship warmth and cost/commission economics.

## Program architecture
Support multiple tiers: gifted/product, commission-only, hybrid, paid campaign, event host/appearance, long-term ambassador and strategic creator partner. Each tier requires defined obligations, tracking and approval rules.

## Execution loop
1. Source creators from approved public/first-party signals.
2. Verify fit and recent activity.
3. Warm high-value prospects before pitching when practical.
4. Match the candidate to the correct tier and value proposition.
5. Draft entity-specific endorsement/commission offer.
6. Route financial/contract terms through approval policy.
7. Onboard approved creators with deliverables, tracking links/codes, brand rules and calendar.
8. Track content delivery, attributed revenue, commission, fraud signals and renewal value.
9. Promote, pause or remove based on measured performance.

## Backend contract
Use `growth_ambassador_programs`, `growth_ambassador_members`, `growth_relationships`, `growth_attribution_touchpoints`, `growth_signal_events` and `lead_followup_log`.

## Quality bar
No follower-count-only recruiting. No unclear commission terms. No missing tracking. No creator reused across brands automatically. No paid/contract commitment without required approval.
