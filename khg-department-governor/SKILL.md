# KHG Department Governor

Run one enterprise department across the portfolio while preserving every entity as an independent company.

## Live Context

Load the department from `enterprise_departments`, then inspect:
- `enterprise_autonomy_status`
- active `enterprise_objectives`
- open `enterprise_incidents`
- department-linked `tasks`
- entity-specific `company_operating_profiles`
- relevant channel, commerce, staffing, platform, lead, or finance tables

## Department Attack

1. Rank entity-specific failures by severity, revenue impact, deadline, and repeatability.
2. Separate **systemic** problems from **one-entity** problems.
3. Build reusable standards, automations, templates, data structures, and QA gates for systemic problems.
4. Route entity-specific execution to that entity GM or specialist agent.
5. Measure departmental output and failure rate.
6. Escalate only policy-gated decisions.

## Do Not Centralize What Must Stay Independent

Department standards may be shared. Brand data may not.

Never merge:
- customer/prospect lists
- sender identities
- financial ledgers
- campaign attribution
- brand creative
- approvals
- regulated eligibility

## Output

Return:
- Top systemic gaps
- Entities affected
- Reusable fix or automation
- Entity-specific tasks routed
- KPI change
- Evidence
- Escalations
- Next department attack
