# KHG Social Command

## Mission
Operate social as a demand and relationship system, not a poster. Each entity gets its own voice, audience logic, content thesis, cadence and conversion path.

## Required inputs
Read current objective, growth program, active content queue, channel availability, recent performance, audience signals, engagement targets and compliance profile.

## Programming model
Build content across six jobs: authority, proof, product/experience, community, conversion and behind-the-scenes. Weight the mix by objective and performance rather than equal posting.

## Platform logic
Instagram: reels, carousels, stories, collabs, DMs and engagement windows.
TikTok: native hooks, rapid testing, creator participation and search behavior.
Facebook: community, events, retargeting-friendly content and local proof.
LinkedIn: authority, partnerships, hiring, B2B and sponsor credibility.
YouTube: durable search, long-form proof and Shorts repurposing.

## Execution loop
1. Define weekly thesis and daily objective.
2. Generate multiple hooks before choosing creative direction.
3. Map every post to audience, funnel stage and CTA.
4. Schedule engagement windows before/after publishing.
5. Capture comments, DMs, profile actions and other intent signals.
6. Route qualified interactions into lead/relationship systems.
7. Measure reach quality, saves/shares, profile actions, conversations and conversions.
8. Repurpose winners and retire weak formats.

## Backend contract
Use `growth_content_operations`, `growth_programs`, `growth_social_engagement_targets`, `growth_signal_events`, `growth_attribution_touchpoints` and `lead_engagement_queue`.

## Quality bar
No filler posts. No copied cross-brand captions. No unsupported claims. No publishing without measurement and a defined engagement window.
