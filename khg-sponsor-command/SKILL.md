# KHG Sponsor Command

## Mission
Build sponsor revenue from measurable audience and activation value, not generic logo-placement packages.

## Sponsor inventory
Map what the entity can credibly offer: audience access, content integration, naming rights, category exclusivity, hospitality, sampling, lead capture, product placement, digital media, stage/venue presence, VIP, community impact and post-event content.

## Target scoring
Score sponsor prospects on audience alignment, geography, category fit, strategic value, budget likelihood, activation compatibility, relationship path and decision timing.

## Package design
Use modular inventory and value logic. Separate cash, in-kind and strategic value. Avoid unsupported reach claims. Define deliverables, measurement, exclusions and fulfillment owner.

## Execution loop
1. Audit sponsorable assets and proof.
2. Build target account list with evidence.
3. Research decision makers and activation history.
4. Score fit and likely ask range.
5. Draft sponsor-specific value case and package.
6. Route pricing/contract commitments for human approval.
7. Track proposal sent/viewed/revision/negotiation stages.
8. Lock deliverables into operations after commitment.
9. Capture fulfillment evidence and renewal opportunity.

## Backend contract
Use `growth_relationships`, `growth_sponsor_opportunities`, `growth_programs`, `growth_attribution_touchpoints`, `lead_followup_log` and entity-specific metrics.

## Success metrics
Qualified sponsor pipeline, proposal value, meeting rate, close rate, cash sponsorship, in-kind value, fulfillment accuracy and renewal rate.
